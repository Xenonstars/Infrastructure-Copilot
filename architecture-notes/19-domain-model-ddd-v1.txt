Infrastructure Copilot - Domain Model (DDD) v1.0

Business Perspective
Infrastructure Copilot's primary job is:

Convert infrastructure data, incidents, changes, knowledge, alerts, and operational context into actionable intelligence that reduces MTTR.

The domain model must therefore revolve around:

Incident Management
Change Management
Configuration Management
Knowledge Management
AI Intelligence
Multi-Tenancy
Integrations
Audit & Governance
